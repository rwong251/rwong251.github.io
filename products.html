The portfolio

Five products. Five distinct decision problems.

CAE · ANALYTICAL PROBLEM SOLVING
What is the right way to solve this business problem?

Bring together the analytical capabilities the problem requires—from understanding drivers and forecasting outcomes to allocating resources and testing decisions.

Value: better predictions, explanations, allocations, plans, and decisions without requiring the user to become a technical specialist.

CFI · INVESTMENT DECISIONS
What should we own, how much, and what risk are we taking?

Move from security information and market evidence to research priorities, portfolio construction, strategy testing, and risk measurement.

Value: make better use of scarce research time and portfolio risk capacity.
Explore CFI →
SENTINEL · CORPORATE RISK
If this shock occurs, what will it cost us—and what can we do about it?

Connect risk events to company exposures, operating transmission, financial effects, mitigation choices, and residual risk.

Value: move from risk identification to quantified management action.
Explore Sentinel →
CMI · MARKET INTELLIGENCE
What is changing outside the company, and what does it mean for us?

Bring economic, industry, market, competitor, geopolitical, climate, and other external signals into a coherent business context.

Value: distinguish noise from developments that should change a commercial or strategic decision.
Explore CMI →
AAIDIS · STRATEGIC DECISIONS
What should we do when several uncertain forces and trade-offs interact?

Evaluate consequential strategic alternatives when no single forecast, model, or analysis is sufficient.

Value: improve the structure, timing, and resilience of high-stakes decisions.
