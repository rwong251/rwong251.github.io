Consilium Sentinel · Business Risk Intelligence

Know what a risk could cost your business—and what you can do about it.

Sentinel connects risk events to the company's actual operating structure and financials, traces how shocks can propagate through the business, and helps management compare mitigation strategies and the residual risk that remains.

Measure corporate risk in the language of the business.

Measure the consequence

A broader risk view

The threats facing a company do not fit into one risk register.

Sentinel brings market and financial, supply-chain and operating, geopolitical and regulatory, climate and physical, and AI and digital risks into one business-impact framework. It can evaluate risks independently or examine how related events move together and create chains of consequences.

Market & financialDemand, rates, prices, funding, and other financial pressures.
Supply chain & operationsSupplier, capacity, facility, logistics, and production disruption.
Geopolitical, climate & regulatoryLocation-specific events, policy changes, physical hazards, and external shocks.
AI & digitalEmerging technology, dependency, model, cyber, and digital operating exposures within the same risk framework.

Real-world situations

The question is not only “what could go wrong?”

A critical supplier is disrupted for 60 days.

Which products and facilities are affected? How much revenue and margin are at risk? Does extra inventory, a second supplier, or a sourcing shift reduce the exposure enough to justify the cost?

Move from supplier concentration to quantified mitigation choices.
A hurricane strikes a major operating region.

Which facilities, suppliers, logistics routes, customers, and financial results are exposed? How does the answer change if the company reroutes production or activates contingency capacity?

Connect a physical event to actual business and financial consequences.
An AI or digital dependency fails.

What business processes depend on it? What operating and financial effects follow? Which fallback, redundancy, or control strategy meaningfully reduces the impact?

Treat emerging AI and digital risk as a measurable business exposure, not a separate checklist.

Mitigation is part of the measurement
