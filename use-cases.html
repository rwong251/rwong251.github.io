Decision intelligence for the moments where weak choices become expensive.

These use cases show how AAIDIS supports real executive decisions under uncertainty. Each example is structured as a decision corridor, not a descriptive workflow, because the objective is to improve what leadership chooses to do next.

The pages below follow one disciplined path through each problem. They make the decision gates explicit, show which AAIDIS muscles are activated, and track the economics of continuing, revising, holding, or stopping as conditions evolve.
