Recognizable business situations

Do any of these questions look familiar?

Which machines genuinely need service now?

Maintenance too early wastes labor, parts, and downtime. Maintenance too late risks breakdowns and lost production.

Use richer evidence to focus intervention where failure risk is actually greatest.
Should we cut price—or protect margin?

Price changes affect volume, margin, competitor reactions, brand perception, and future pricing power at the same time.

Evaluate the decision before committing, not after the market responds.
What would a supplier disruption actually cost us?

A risk score does not tell management which products, facilities, revenues, margins, or cash flows are exposed.

Trace shocks through the business and compare mitigation choices.
Which securities deserve our limited research time?

Investment teams face far more opportunities than they can investigate deeply.

Narrow the universe, test ideas, construct portfolios, and understand the risk being accepted.
Why is this market moving—and what does it mean for us?

Prices and demand can change because of costs, supply, policy, competition, weather, consumer behavior, or several forces together.

Connect market developments to the commercial decisions they affect.
Should we expand, wait, acquire, or change direction?

Strategic choices combine uncertain forecasts, competing objectives, external reactions, and asymmetric consequences.

Evaluate alternatives as decisions, not as disconnected analyses.

One decision intelligence vision

Different problems need different tools—and sometimes several tools working together.

A forecast can tell you what may happen. It may not tell you why, what action to take, or how that action performs when conditions change. Consilium.ai products are designed to combine the capabilities required by the problem so professionals can move beyond analysis toward better decisions.
