CFI · Financial Intelligence

Move from market information to better investment decisions.

CFI helps investment professionals decide what deserves attention, what belongs in the portfolio, how much to own, whether a strategy holds up under testing, and what risk the portfolio is actually taking.

Investment decision chain
Universe → research → security selection → portfolio construction → strategy testing → risk → action

The value is not another isolated signal. It is connecting analysis to the portfolio decision.

Investment situations

Where investment teams need more than a screen or a chart.

We have 2,000 securities. Which 40 deserve deeper work?

Use the information and criteria relevant to the mandate to narrow the universe and focus scarce research time where it has the highest potential value.

Business value: improve the productivity of the investment research process.
Which portfolio best expresses our view?

A list of attractive securities does not determine position sizes. CFI can incorporate expected return, risk, diversification, mandate limits, liquidity, and other portfolio constraints.

Business value: translate security views into a portfolio that reflects the investor's actual objectives and constraints.
Did the strategy work—or did the market regime flatter it?

Backtest a proposed strategy, examine performance across periods and conditions, and see where the apparent edge weakens or disappears.

Business value: reduce the risk of committing capital to a strategy that only looks robust in one historical window.
What happens if rates, spreads, volatility, or markets move sharply?

Measure downside exposure and test how the portfolio behaves under alternative market conditions before the shock becomes real.

Business value: understand the risk being accepted before it becomes a realized loss.

Integrated investment workflow

Fundamental, market, quantitative, portfolio, and risk views belong in the same decision process.

CFI is designed so the investor can supply the security universe and the relevant company, market, macroeconomic, geopolitical, climate, and other information needed for the analysis—then use the appropriate combination of tools for security selection, portfolio construction, backtesting, risk measurement, and return forecasting.

Make the portfolio decision—not just the analysis.

Use CFI to connect research, security selection, construction, testing, and risk into one investment workflow.
